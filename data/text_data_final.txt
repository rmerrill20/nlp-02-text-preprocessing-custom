Horror is a film genre that seeks to elicit physical or psychological fear in its viewers.
Horror films often explore dark subject matter and may deal with transgressive topics or themes.
Broad elements of the genre include monsters, apocalyptic events, and religious or folk beliefs.
Horror films have existed since the early 20th century.
Early inspirations predating film include folklore;
the religious beliefs and superstitions of different cultures;
and the Goth and horror literature of authors such as Edgar Allan Poe,
Bram Stoker, and Mary Shelley.
